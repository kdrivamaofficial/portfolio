Customer Service in AI Era

A talking-head explainer on customer service in the AI era — shot on a smartphone, edited end-to-end by AI, delivered in one day.

A talking-head explainer on what customer service looks like in the AI era — produced entirely through an AI-assisted workflow, from raw smartphone footage to finished cut in a single day.

Watch the video ↓

Tools Used

  • Claude — used to edit the footage, including trimming, motion graphics, audio treatment, and post-production.
  • Google Flow — used to generate supporting B-roll with the Omni model.
  1. Filmed on a smartphone. The talking-head segment was recorded with nothing more than a phone camera — no studio, no crew, no dedicated gear.
  2. Raw footage uploaded to Claude. The unedited recording went straight into Claude as the working source material.
  3. Editing skills installed on Claude. A set of purpose-built skills was added to give Claude the editing capabilities the project needed.
  4. Claude performed the edit. Trimming, motion graphics, audio treatment, and the rest of the post-production pass were all handled by Claude.
  5. B-roll generated with Google Flow. Supporting shots were produced using Google Flow running the Omni model.
  6. Ready within one day. After a handful of revision rounds, the finished video was delivered inside a single day.

Why this matters for fast-paced teams

This workflow compresses a traditional multi-day production cycle into a single day, without a studio, a crew, or specialist editing software. For companies operating in a fast-paced environment — where training content, product updates, and internal comms need to ship quickly - it makes high-quality video a repeatable, low-overhead process rather than a project.
