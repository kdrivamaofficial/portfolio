Creative Process

  1. Record the raw video. The walkthrough was captured directly from the screen to preserve the real sequence of actions and system navigation.
  2. Edit the layout. The recording was organized for clarity using Tella AI and manual editing to tighten the flow.
  3. Add zoom effect to main points. Key steps were emphasized with zoom-ins so the viewer can follow the exact navigation and action points.
  4. Highlights effect for important note. Critical notes and support guidance were visually emphasized to improve retention.
  5. Remove fillers and cut unnecessary elaboration. Extra pauses and repetition were trimmed so the tutorial stays focused on the operational workflow.

What the tutorial covers

A practical walkthrough of how Best Practices, Backend systems, and the Knowledge Base connect in day-to-day customer support workflows.
